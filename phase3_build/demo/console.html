How this page works (read once)

You are looking at the whole system on your own computer. Left: a truck driver's WhatsApp. Right: the warehouse staff's screen. The agent sits between them: it reads the driver's messages, offers dock slots, and sends booking requests. Only the warehouse, or a rule the warehouse authorised, can confirm a booking.

Pick a flow below, then press Run this step again and again. Each step says what is happening in real life, does it, and checks the result (✓ as expected, ✗ not as expected, ℹ for information). Watch the phone and the console below change as you go. At the end, press Pass or Fail. Your verdict is saved.

Nothing here touches WhatsApp, AWS or real trucks. The AI is replaced by simple rules, so there is no cost.
